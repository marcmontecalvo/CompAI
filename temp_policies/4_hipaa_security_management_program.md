## Notes for Compliance Writer

This policy is critical as it establishes the foundation for the entire HIPAA security program. Key considerations:

1. **Security Officer**: Must be a designated individual with sufficient authority and resources
2. **Risk Assessment**: Must be comprehensive, documented, and actionable
3. **Addressable Specifications**: Decision framework must be clear and defensible
4. **Documentation**: All decisions and assessments must be thoroughly documented for audit purposes
5. **Integration**: This policy ties together all other HIPAA security policies

Recommend reviewing HHS Security Rule guidance and OCR audit protocols when drafting content.
