## Policy Statements

**[TO BE COMPLETED - See guidance below]**

### Required Sections:

#### 1. Security Incident Definition
- Unauthorized access, use, disclosure, modification, or destruction of ePHI
- Successful or attempted attacks on ePHI systems
- Violations of security policies involving ePHI
- Malware, ransomware, or hacking incidents affecting ePHI
- Lost or stolen devices containing ePHI
- Improper ePHI disposal or disclosure
- Business associate security incidents

#### 2. Incident Reporting Requirements
- All workforce members must report suspected security incidents immediately
- Reporting channels (IT Security, HIPAA Security Officer, hotline)
- No retaliation for good-faith reporting
- Business associate reporting obligations (via BAA)
- Timeline: Report within 1 hour for critical incidents, 4 hours for others

#### 3. Incident Response Team
- Core team members (HIPAA Security Officer, Privacy Officer, IT Security, Legal)
- Extended team (PR, HR, Executive Leadership)
- On-call rotation and escalation
- Team training and preparedness
- Authority and decision-making

#### 4. Incident Response Process
- Detection and reporting
- Initial assessment and triage (severity classification)
- Containment (isolate affected systems, revoke access)
- Investigation (root cause, scope, affected ePHI)
- Eradication (remove threat, patch vulnerabilities)
- Recovery (restore systems, validate security)
- Post-incident review (lessons learned, corrective actions)

#### 5. ePHI Breach Determination (4-Part Test)
1. **Acquisition, Access, Use, or Disclosure**: Did the incident involve ePHI impermissibly acquired, accessed, used, or disclosed?
2. **Not Authorized by HIPAA Privacy Rule**: Was the acquisition, access, use, or disclosure not permitted under the Privacy Rule?
3. **Compromises Security or Privacy**: Does it compromise the security or privacy of the ePHI?
4. **Low Probability Exception**: Is there a low probability that the ePHI has been compromised? (consider 4 factors: nature/extent, who accessed, whether actually acquired/viewed, technology mitigations)

- If YES to 1-3 and NO to 4 = BREACH requiring notification
- Document breach determination decision with rationale

#### 6. Breach Notification to Individuals
- **Timeline**: Within 60 days of breach discovery
- **Method**: Written notice by first-class mail (or email if individual agreed to electronic)
- **Content**: Brief incident description, types of information involved, steps individuals should take, what organization is doing, contact information
- **Substitute Notice**: If contact information insufficient/outdated for 10+ individuals, use substitute notice (website + media notice or toll-free number)

#### 7. Breach Notification to HHS (Office for Civil Rights)
- **Breaches affecting 500+ individuals**: Within 60 days of discovery, concurrent with individual notification
- **Breaches affecting <500 individuals**: Annual notification (within 60 days of calendar year end)
- **Submission**: HHS Breach Portal (https://ocrportal.hhs.gov/ocr/breach)
- **Content**: Organization name, number of individuals affected, brief description

#### 8. Breach Notification to Media (if applicable)
- **Requirement**: If breach affects 500+ individuals in a single State or jurisdiction
- **Timeline**: Within 60 days of discovery
- **Method**: Prominent media outlets serving the affected jurisdiction
- **Content**: Same as individual notice content

#### 9. Business Associate Breach Notification (from BA to CE)
- Business associates must notify {{COMPANY}} within 30 days of breach discovery (per BAA)
- {{COMPANY}} is responsible for individual, HHS, and media notification
- Business associate provides all information needed for notifications
- {{COMPANY}} may require business associate corrective actions

#### 10. Incident Documentation
- Incident log with all incidents (breach and non-breach)
- Investigation notes and findings
- Breach determination documentation (4-part test analysis)
- Notification records (dates, recipients, method)
- Risk assessment for "low probability" determination
- Corrective actions and remediation
- 6-year retention of all incident and breach documentation

## Procedures

**[TO BE COMPLETED]**

Should include detailed procedures for:

### Security Incident Reporting and Initial Response
1. Workforce member detects or suspects security incident
2. Immediate report to IT Security via [phone/email/portal]
3. IT Security logs incident in incident management system
4. Initial triage (severity: critical, high, medium, low)
5. Escalate to HIPAA Security Officer if ePHI potentially involved
6. Assemble incident response team if critical
7. Begin containment within 1 hour for critical incidents

### ePHI Breach Investigation and Determination
1. Incident investigation to determine scope
2. Identify all ePHI involved (data elements, individuals affected)
3. Determine who accessed/acquired ePHI (authorized or unauthorized)
4. Apply 4-part breach determination test
5. Document breach determination decision with evidence
6. If breach: Activate breach notification procedures
7. If not breach: Document rationale and close incident
8. Privacy Officer and Legal Counsel review and concur

### Breach Notification to Individuals (500+ individuals)
1. Breach determination confirmed
2. Draft breach notification letter (Legal + Privacy Officer)
3. Compile affected individuals list with addresses
4. Executive approval of notification
5. Mail notifications via first-class mail within 60 days
6. Submit to HHS Breach Portal concurrently
7. Issue media notification to prominent outlets in affected states
8. Establish call center/hotline for individual questions
9. Document all notifications (dates, counts, method)
10. Monitor for follow-up questions and OCR inquiry

### Breach Notification to Individuals (<500 individuals)
1. Breach determination confirmed
2. Draft breach notification letter
3. Executive approval
4. Mail notifications within 60 days
5. Log breach for annual HHS reporting
6. Document notification
7. Annual report to HHS (within 60 days of year-end)

### Business Associate Breach Notification (BA to CE)
1. Business associate discovers breach involving {{COMPANY}} ePHI
2. Business associate notifies {{COMPANY}} within 30 days (per BAA)
3. {{COMPANY}} Privacy Officer reviews BA notification
4. {{COMPANY}} conducts breach determination
5. {{COMPANY}} issues individual, HHS, and media notifications (if applicable)
6. {{COMPANY}} may require BA corrective action plan
7. {{COMPANY}} evaluates ongoing BA relationship

### Post-Incident Review
1. Schedule post-incident review within 7 days of incident closure
2. Assemble incident response team
3. Review incident timeline and response actions
4. Identify what went well and areas for improvement
5. Determine root cause
6. Develop corrective actions (technical, process, training)
7. Assign corrective action ownership and deadlines
8. Track corrective actions to completion
9. Update incident response plan if needed
10. Document lessons learned

## Incident Severity Classification

**[TO BE COMPLETED]**

Example severity classification:

### Critical (Immediate Response)
- Ransomware or active cyberattack
- Large-scale ePHI exfiltration
- Loss of critical ePHI system availability
- Breach affecting 500+ individuals

### High (Response within 4 hours)
- Unauthorized ePHI access by workforce
- Lost/stolen device with unencrypted ePHI
- Breach affecting 100-499 individuals
- Malware infection on ePHI system

### Medium (Response within 24 hours)
- Policy violation involving ePHI
- Breach affecting 10-99 individuals
- Suspicious activity on ePHI system

### Low (Response within 72 hours)
- Breach affecting <10 individuals
- False positive security alerts
- Minor policy violations

## Notes for Compliance Writer

Breach notification is one of the highest-risk areas of HIPAA compliance (significant financial penalties). Key considerations:

1. **60-Day Timeline**: Strictly enforced by OCR - calendar days, not business days
2. **4-Part Test**: Document breach determination rigorously; OCR will review if investigated
3. **Low Probability Exception**: Rare and difficult to prove; most incidents are breaches
4. **Harm Threshold Removed**: 2013 Omnibus Rule removed "harm" threshold; focus on risk of compromise
5. **Business Associate Breaches**: Covered entity is ultimately responsible for notification even if BA caused breach
6. **OCR Breach Portal**: Public website - breach notifications are visible to media and public
7. **State Laws**: Some states have additional breach notification requirements (coordinate with Legal)
8. **Breach Fatigue**: Organizations with frequent breaches face increased OCR scrutiny
9. **Remediation**: OCR expects detailed corrective action plans for all breaches

This policy must be closely coordinated with Legal Counsel and Privacy Officer. Recommend legal review before finalization.
