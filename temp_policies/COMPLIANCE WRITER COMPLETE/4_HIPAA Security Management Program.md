# **HIPAA Security Management Program**

Policy Type: HIPAA Security Policy  
Frequency: Yearly Review  
Department: IT  
Status: ✅ COMPLETE

## **Coverage**

This policy consolidates requirements for the following HIPAA Security Rule controls:

* **Control 5**: HIPAA Security Officer & Assigned Responsibility  
* **Control 6**: HIPAA Security Evaluation & Risk Assessment  
* **Control 18**: HIPAA Addressable Specification Assessment  
* **Control 21**: HIPAA Policies & Procedures Documentation  
* **Policy Compliance** (general administrative requirements)

## **Purpose**

To establish the formal, documented framework for the management, implementation, and maintenance of the HIPAA Security Rule compliance program within {{COMPANY}}. This policy ensures that security activities, including risk management, compliance monitoring, and policy documentation, are assigned, conducted, and reviewed on an ongoing basis to protect electronic Protected Health Information (ePHI).

## **Scope**

This policy governs:

* The designation, authority, and responsibilities of the **HIPAA Security Officer** and supporting roles.  
* The processes for conducting the **Annual Security Risk Assessment** and subsequent risk management activities.  
* The methodology for evaluating and documenting **Addressable Specifications** of the HIPAA Security Rule.  
* The standards for creating, approving, managing, and retaining all HIPAA-related policies and procedures.

## **Policy Statements**

### **1\. HIPAA Security Officer Designation**

The **HIPAA Security Officer (HSO)** shall be officially appointed by Executive Leadership and shall possess the necessary authority and resources to fulfill all required Security Rule functions. The HSO reports to the Chief Information Security Officer (CISO) or Chief Executive Officer (CEO) and is accountable for the overall security management process and compliance with this policy.

### **2\. Risk Analysis and Management**

{{COMPANY}} shall conduct a formal, comprehensive **Security Risk Assessment (SRA)** annually, or whenever significant system or organizational changes occur. The SRA shall:

* Utilize a documented methodology (e.g., based on NIST SP 800-30).  
* Identify and document potential threats and vulnerabilities to ePHI systems.  
* Assess the likelihood and potential impact of threat occurrence to determine risk levels.  
* Document a **Risk Mitigation Plan** to address all identified risks, prioritizing those deemed **{{CRITICAL}}** or High.  
* Require formal **Residual Risk Acceptance** by the HSO or Executive Leadership for risks that cannot be fully mitigated.

### **3\. Security Evaluation**

{{COMPANY}} shall periodically and in response to environmental or operational changes, conduct a **Security Evaluation** of its security policies, procedures, and technical controls. Evaluation triggers include new technology adoption, facility changes, major software upgrades, or changes in ownership. The evaluation methodology shall be objective, and findings shall be documented, tracked, and remediated via the Corrective Action Plan (CAP) process.

### **4\. Addressable Specification Assessment**

The HSO shall review all **Addressable Implementation Specifications** of the HIPAA Security Rule at least annually. For each addressable specification, a formal, documented decision shall be made and justified:

* **Implement**: The specification is implemented as described.  
* **Implement Alternative**: An alternative security measure is implemented that achieves an equivalent effect. The alternative must be documented and justified.  
* **Do Not Implement**: A decision not to implement, along with a documented justification based on a formal risk assessment demonstrating the measure is not reasonable or appropriate.

### **5\. Policy Documentation Requirements**

All security policies and procedures shall adhere to the following documentation standards:

* **Approval Workflow**: Require formal approval by the designated Policy Owner and Executive Leadership prior to implementation.  
* **Version Control**: Maintain strict version control, revision history, and effective dates.  
* **Retention**: Policies and related documentation (e.g., risk assessments, training records) shall be retained for a minimum of **6 years** from the date of their creation or the date they were last in effect, whichever is later.  
* **Review and Update**: All policies must be reviewed at least annually or immediately following a significant security incident or regulatory change.

### **6\. Compliance Monitoring**

The HSO and Compliance team shall establish procedures for the ongoing monitoring of security controls and policy compliance through:

* **Internal Audits**: Periodic assessments of technical controls and administrative procedures.  
* **Corrective Action Tracking**: Managing and monitoring the completion of all CAPs resulting from risk assessments, internal audits, and evaluations.  
* **Management Reporting**: Quarterly reporting of security posture, risk status, and compliance metrics to Executive Leadership.

## **Procedures**

### **Annual Security Risk Assessment Workflow**

1. **Scope Definition**: HSO defines the scope, systems, and processes to be included in the SRA (ePHI boundary).  
2. **Data Collection**: Collect technical documentation, asset inventory, configuration data, and conduct interviews with key personnel.  
3. **Threat & Vulnerability Analysis**: Identify credible threats and known vulnerabilities (internal/external) across all scoped systems.  
4. **Risk Determination**: Assess likelihood and impact using the defined risk scoring matrix.  
5. **Mitigation Plan Development**: Create an actionable plan for all High/Medium risks with assigned owners and deadlines.  
6. **Executive Review**: Present SRA findings and the Mitigation Plan to Executive Leadership for formal review and signature/acceptance.  
7. **Documentation**: Retain SRA report, mitigation plan, and acceptance documentation for **6 years**.

### **Addressable Specification Assessment Procedure**

1. **Annual Review**: HSO and IT Security review the full list of 36 Addressable Specifications.  
2. **Implementation Status**: Determine current status (implemented, alternative, not implemented).  
3. **Justification**: For any specification not fully implemented, document the business justification and conduct a risk assessment to justify the decision.  
4. **Documentation**: Complete the official **Addressable Specification Documentation Matrix**, ensuring the rationale is clear and defensible for auditors.  
5. **Review**: HSO reviews and formally signs off on the matrix.

### **Policy Exception Handling**

1. **Request Submission**: Workforce member or manager submits a formal **Policy Exception Request** to the HSO.  
2. **Risk Assessment**: The HSO and IT Security assess the risk introduced by the exception, identifying compensating controls.  
3. **Approval**: If the risk is acceptable with compensating controls, the HSO approves the exception. High-risk exceptions require Executive Leadership sign-off.  
4. **Documentation**: The exception, risk assessment, compensating controls, and approval are documented and reviewed annually.
