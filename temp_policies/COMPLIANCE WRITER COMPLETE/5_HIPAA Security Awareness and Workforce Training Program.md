# **HIPAA Security Awareness and Workforce Training Program**

Policy Type: HIPAA Security Policy  
Frequency: Yearly Review  
Department: HR  
Status: ✅ COMPLETE

## **Coverage**

This policy consolidates requirements for the following HIPAA Security Rule controls:

* **Control 2**: HIPAA Security Awareness & Workforce Training  
* Elements of **Control 3**: Workforce Security & Sanction Policy (training components)  
* Elements of **Control 15**: ePHI Access Authorization & Role-Based Controls (training requirements)

## **Purpose**

To establish comprehensive security awareness and training requirements to ensure that all workforce members understand their roles and responsibilities in safeguarding electronic Protected Health Information (ePHI) and complying with the HIPAA Security Rule. This program is mandatory for mitigating risks posed by human error and social engineering.

## **Scope**

This policy applies to:

* All workforce members, including **employees, contractors, volunteers, trainees, and temporary staff**, regardless of their access level to ePHI.  
* **New hires** during the onboarding phase.  
* Workforce members whose **role changes** require new or different access to ePHI.  
* The **tracking, documentation, and retention** of all security training records.

## **Policy Statements**

### **1\. Mandatory Annual Training**

All workforce members must complete HIPAA Security Rule training **annually**.

* **Completion Requirement**: 100% workforce completion is mandatory.  
* **Content**: Training shall cover Security Rule fundamentals, organizational policies for handling ePHI, sanction policies, incident reporting procedures, and current threats (e.g., ransomware, phishing).  
* **Deadline**: All annual training must be completed within the calendar year, by **December 31st**, or within a 30-day window upon notification.  
* **Consequences**: Failure to complete mandatory annual training within the deadline will result in disciplinary action up to and including **suspension of ePHI access**, per the Sanction Policy.

### **2\. New Hire Training**

New workforce members must complete initial security training within **30 days** of their start date.

* **Access Restriction**: New hires are **prohibited from accessing ePHI** or systems containing ePHI until mandatory training is successfully completed and attested.  
* **Onboarding**: Training verification shall be integrated into the new hire onboarding checklist and verified by the HR/Training department.

### **3\. Role-Specific Training**

Workforce members with privileged or administrative access to ePHI systems, or those in clinical roles, shall receive additional training.

* **Privileged Access**: Focus on unique user IDs, strong password/MFA usage, configuration management, and the protection of administrator credentials.  
* **Department-Specific**: Training may be tailored to address the unique risks and procedures of handling ePHI within specific departments (e.g., patient registration, billing, clinical charting).  
* **Business Associates**: Business associates are required via the BAA to train their own workforce; {{COMPANY}} may require proof of training completion upon request.

### **4\. Security Awareness Program**

{{COMPANY}} shall maintain an ongoing Security Awareness Program to reinforce training concepts and address emerging threats.

* **Communications**: Monthly or quarterly security reminders shall be distributed via email, intranet, or physical postings.  
* **Phishing Simulation**: Phishing and social engineering simulation exercises shall be conducted quarterly to assess workforce vigilance and provide targeted training to those who fail the exercise.  
* **Reminders**: Awareness campaigns shall cover topics like the **Clean Desk Policy**, proper workstation locking, and physical security reminders.

### **5\. Malicious Software Awareness**

Training shall include awareness of malicious software and social engineering techniques.

* **Recognition**: Workforce members must be trained to recognize and avoid phishing emails, suspicious links, and unsolicited attachments.  
* **Safe Practices**: Training covers safe internet usage, prohibition of unauthorized software installation, and proper handling of removable media (e.g., USB drives).  
* **Reporting**: All suspicious activity, including failed phishing attempts or potential malware infections, must be reported to IT Security **immediately**.

### **6\. Login Monitoring and Password Management**

Workforce members shall be trained on protecting their login credentials and recognizing unauthorized access attempts.

* **Password Protection**: Training emphasizes the creation of strong, unique passwords and the **absolute prohibition of password sharing**.  
* **MFA**: Workforce members must be trained on the proper use of multi-factor authentication (MFA) tools where required.  
* **Workstation Security**: Training reinforces the requirement to **lock the workstation** before leaving the desk and recognizing signs of unauthorized login monitoring.

### **7\. Training Documentation**

The HR/Training department shall ensure all security training is properly documented.

* **Records**: Training records must include the workforce member's name, completion date, course name, content outline, and method of delivery (e.g., certificate of completion).  
* **Retention**: All training documentation and completion records must be retained for a minimum of **6 years** from the date of creation, as required by HIPAA.  
* **Attestation**: Workforce members must formally attest to understanding and agreeing to comply with the policies covered in the training.

## **Responsibilities**

| Role | Responsibility |
| :---- | :---- |
| **HR / Training Department** | Administering the training program, managing the Learning Management System (LMS), tracking completion, and generating compliance reports. |
| **HIPAA Security Officer** | Overseeing the content of the security training, coordinating the annual review process, and managing non-compliance escalations. |
| **Department Managers** | Ensuring 100% completion by all team members and enforcing disciplinary action for non-compliance. |
| **IT Security Team** | Providing technical expertise, designing malicious software awareness content, and conducting phishing simulations. |

| **Individual Workforce Members** | Completing all assigned training by the required deadlines and attesting to compliance. |

## **Procedures**

### **Annual Training Deployment and Tracking**

1. **Content Update**: HSO and Privacy Officer review and update annual training content to reflect policy changes, new threats, and lessons learned from past incidents.  
2. **Deployment**: HR deploys the annual training module via the LMS by October 1st.  
3. **Monitoring**: The LMS automatically tracks completion rates; reminders are sent weekly to non-compliant users starting 60 days before the deadline.  
4. **Non-Compliance Escalation**: Within 7 days of the final deadline, the HSO and HR notify managers of non-compliant employees. Managers enforce disciplinary actions per the sanction policy, including suspending ePHI access.  
5. **Reporting**: HR provides a final annual completion report to the HSO and Executive Leadership.

### **New Hire Training Workflow**

1. **Onboarding**: HR assigns the initial HIPAA Security/Privacy training module on the new hire's start date.  
2. **Pre-Access Check**: The IT/IAM team verifies training completion via the LMS before granting the new hire access to any ePHI systems.  
3. **Verification**: Manager confirms training completion before the new hire is assigned tasks involving ePHI.  
4. **Documentation**: Completion is logged in the LMS and retained for **6 years**.

### **Security Awareness Campaign Execution**

1. **Planning**: The IT Security Team and HSO plan quarterly or monthly awareness topics (e.g., "Phishing Awareness Month," "Clean Desk Checkup").  
2. **Execution**: Distribute reminders via internal channels. Conduct phishing simulation exercises targeting a random sample of the workforce.  
3. **Remediation Training**: Workforce members who click on a phishing link are immediately assigned mandatory, remedial training modules specific to phishing recognition.
