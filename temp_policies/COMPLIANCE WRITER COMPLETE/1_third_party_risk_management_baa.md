# Third-Party Risk Management & Business Associate Agreements

**Policy Type**: HIPAA Security Policy
**Frequency**: Yearly Review
**Department**: Admin
**Status**: ✅ COMPLETE

---

## Purpose

To ensure that {{COMPANY}} maintains appropriate Business Associate Agreements (BAAs) with all third-party vendors, contractors, and business associates who create, receive, maintain, or transmit electronic Protected Health Information (ePHI) on behalf of {{COMPANY}}. This policy establishes procedures for vendor risk assessment, BAA execution and management, and ongoing monitoring of business associate compliance with HIPAA Security Rule requirements.

---

## Scope

This policy applies to:
- All third-party vendors with access to ePHI
- Business associates as defined by HIPAA
- Subcontractors of business associates
- Cloud service providers hosting ePHI
- IT vendors with system-level access to ePHI environments

---

## Policy Statements

### 1. Business Associate Identification

{{COMPANY}} shall maintain a comprehensive inventory of all business associates and vendors with ePHI access, including:
- Vendor name and contact information
- Type of ePHI accessed or processed
- Services provided
- BAA execution date and expiration date
- Subcontractor relationships

### 2. Risk Assessment Requirements

Before engaging any vendor with ePHI access, {{COMPANY}} shall:
- Conduct vendor security risk assessment
- Evaluate vendor HIPAA compliance capabilities
- Review vendor security certifications (SOC 2, HITRUST, ISO 27001)
- Assess vendor incident response procedures
- Verify vendor breach notification processes

### 3. BAA Execution

All business associates must execute a compliant BAA before accessing ePHI. The BAA shall include:
- Permitted uses and disclosures of ePHI
- Safeguard requirements (administrative, physical, technical)
- Breach notification obligations
- Subcontractor BAA flow-down requirements
- Audit and inspection rights
- Termination procedures and ePHI return/destruction

### 4. Subcontractor Management

Business associates who engage subcontractors with ePHI access must:
- Obtain written authorization from {{COMPANY}}
- Execute BAAs with all subcontractors
- Provide {{COMPANY}} with subcontractor BAA documentation
- Monitor subcontractor HIPAA compliance

### 5. Ongoing Monitoring

{{COMPANY}} shall conduct ongoing monitoring of business associate compliance:
- Quarterly BAA inventory review
- Annual vendor security assessments
- Review of vendor security incident reports
- Validation of vendor HIPAA training programs
- Monitoring vendor breach notifications

### 6. BAA Renewals and Amendments

{{COMPANY}} shall:
- Track BAA expiration dates
- Initiate renewal process 90 days before expiration
- Execute amendments when regulatory requirements change
- Document all BAA modifications

### 7. Termination Procedures

Upon BAA termination, business associates shall:
- Return or destroy all ePHI within 30 days
- Provide written certification of ePHI destruction
- Revoke all system access credentials
- Complete exit procedures per BAA terms

## Responsibilities

### Privacy Officer / Compliance Team
- Maintain BAA inventory
- Coordinate vendor risk assessments
- Execute and track BAAs
- Monitor business associate compliance
- Manage BAA renewals

### IT Security Team
- Conduct vendor technical security assessments
- Review vendor security controls
- Monitor vendor access to ePHI systems
- Validate vendor encryption and authentication

### Legal / Contracts Team
- Review and negotiate BAA terms
- Ensure regulatory compliance of BAA language
- Manage BAA amendments and renewals
- Handle vendor contract disputes

### Department Managers
- Identify vendors requiring BAAs
- Notify compliance team of new vendor engagements
- Report business associate security concerns
- Participate in vendor risk assessments

---

## Procedures

### Vendor Onboarding with ePHI Access

1. **Vendor Identification**: Department identifies need for vendor with ePHI access
2. **Risk Assessment**: Compliance team conducts vendor risk assessment
3. **Security Review**: IT security team reviews vendor security controls
4. **BAA Execution**: Legal/compliance team executes BAA before ePHI access
5. **Inventory Update**: Add vendor to BAA inventory with key dates
6. **Access Provisioning**: IT provisions access only after BAA execution

### Quarterly BAA Inventory Review

1. **Pull Current Inventory**: Retrieve all active BAAs from contract management system
2. **Verify Status**: Confirm all active vendors with ePHI access have current BAAs
3. **Check Expirations**: Identify BAAs expiring within 90 days
4. **Initiate Renewals**: Contact vendors with upcoming expirations
5. **Validate Subcontractors**: Review subcontractor BAA flow-down documentation
6. **Report Findings**: Document review results and remediation actions

### Business Associate Security Incident Response

1. **Incident Notification**: Business associate notifies {{COMPANY}} of security incident
2. **Impact Assessment**: Evaluate if incident constitutes HIPAA breach
3. **Containment**: Work with business associate to contain incident
4. **Investigation**: Review business associate investigation report
5. **Breach Determination**: Determine if breach notification required ({{COMPANY}} responsibility)
6. **Corrective Action**: Require business associate corrective action plan
7. **Monitoring**: Enhanced monitoring of business associate post-incident
