## How to Use These Templates

### For COMPLETE Policies (1-3):

1. **Review**: Have legal counsel and compliance team review content
2. **Customize**: Replace {{COMPANY}} and {{CRITICAL}} placeholders with actual values
3. **Tailor**: Adjust procedures and standards to fit your organization's size and complexity
4. **Approve**: Obtain executive approval and sign-off
5. **Distribute**: Publish to workforce via document management system
6. **Train**: Include in security awareness training

### For INCOMPLETE Policies (4-10):

1. **Review Structure**: Compliance writer reviews template structure and guidance notes
2. **Research**: Review HHS guidance, OCR audit protocols, and industry best practices
3. **Draft Content**: Complete the policy statements, procedures, and standards sections
4. **Internal Review**: IT, HR, Legal review draft content
5. **Finalize**: Incorporate feedback and complete document control sections
6. **Approval**: Obtain executive sign-off
7. **Publish**: Distribute to workforce

### Special Note on Policy #10 (Group Health Plan):

**IMPORTANT**: Determine applicability first before spending time on this policy. Most organizations are NOT subject to this requirement. See the policy file for detailed applicability decision tree.

## Integration with Existing Policies

If your organization already has policies covering some of these areas (e.g., general security policies, access control policies), you have two options:

### Option 1: Consolidate
- Merge HIPAA-specific requirements into existing policies
- Add HIPAA references and controls to current policy framework
- Ensure all HIPAA requirements are explicitly addressed

### Option 2: Separate
- Maintain HIPAA policies separately
- Cross-reference between HIPAA policies and general security policies
- Clearly identify which policies apply to ePHI specifically

Most organizations choose Option 1 (consolidate) to avoid policy duplication and simplify workforce training.
