# HIPAA Security Awareness and Workforce Training Program

**Policy Type**: HIPAA Security Policy
**Frequency**: Yearly Review
**Department**: HR
**Status**: ⚠️ NEEDS COMPLIANCE WRITER

## Coverage

This policy consolidates requirements for the following HIPAA Security Rule controls:
- **Control 2**: HIPAA Security Awareness & Workforce Training
- Elements of **Control 3**: Workforce Security & Sanction Policy (training components)
- Elements of **Control 15**: ePHI Access Authorization & Role-Based Controls (training requirements)

---

## Purpose

**[POLICY CONTENT TO BE COMPLETED BY COMPLIANCE WRITER]**

This policy should establish comprehensive security awareness and training requirements including:
- Mandatory annual HIPAA Security Rule training for all workforce members
- New hire training requirements (within 30 days)
- Role-specific training for privileged access
- Security reminders and awareness campaigns
- Protection from malicious software training
- Login monitoring and password management awareness
- Security incident reporting training
- Training documentation and completion tracking

---

## Scope

**[TO BE COMPLETED]**

Should apply to:
- All workforce members (employees, contractors, volunteers)
- Business associates (encouraged/required via BAA)
- New hires within first 30 days
- Workforce members with role changes affecting ePHI access
- Temporary and contingent workers

## Policy Statements

**[TO BE COMPLETED - See guidance below]**

### Required Sections:

#### 1. Mandatory Annual Training
- 100% workforce completion requirement
- Training content requirements (Security Rule, ePHI handling, incident reporting)
- Training delivery methods (LMS, in-person, virtual)
- Completion deadlines
- Tracking and reporting
- Consequences for non-completion

#### 2. New Hire Training
- Training within 30 days of hire
- No ePHI access until training completed
- New hire onboarding checklist integration
- Verification and documentation

#### 3. Role-Specific Training
- Additional training for privileged/administrative access
- Department-specific ePHI handling procedures
- Clinical vs. administrative workforce differentiation
- Vendor/contractor training requirements

#### 4. Security Awareness Program
- Monthly security awareness communications
- Phishing simulation exercises
- Password security campaigns
- Physical security reminders
- Incident reporting awareness

#### 5. Malicious Software Awareness
- Recognizing phishing and social engineering
- Safe email and internet practices
- USB and removable media risks
- Ransomware awareness
- Reporting suspicious activity

#### 6. Login Monitoring and Password Management
- Password creation and protection guidance
- No password sharing policy
- Multi-factor authentication training
- Recognizing unauthorized access attempts
- Workstation security practices

#### 7. Training Documentation
- Training completion records (name, date, course, certificate)
- LMS integration and tracking
- Audit-ready reporting
- 6-year retention of training records

## Notes for Compliance Writer

This policy is essential for demonstrating workforce competency in HIPAA security. Key considerations:

1. **100% Completion**: OCR expects 100% workforce training completion - no exceptions
2. **New Hire Timeline**: 30-day completion window is industry standard; no ePHI access before training
3. **Documentation**: Training records must be maintained for 6 years per HIPAA
4. **LMS Integration**: Automated tracking via LMS significantly reduces administrative burden
5. **Refresher Content**: Annual training should include updates on new threats, incidents, policy changes
6. **Attestation**: Consider requiring workforce attestation that they understand and will comply
7. **Sanctions**: Reference sanction policy for non-compliance

Coordinate with HR on integration with existing onboarding and annual compliance training programs.
